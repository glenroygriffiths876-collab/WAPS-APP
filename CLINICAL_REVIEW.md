# Clinical Review
WAPS deliberately avoids diagnosis, standardized test reproduction and claims that app scores equal clinical improvement. It supports multimodal communication, caregiver responsiveness, AAC availability, self-advocacy, prompting awareness and real-world generalization.

**External validation required before public clinical release:** review by qualified SLP/AAC professionals; Jamaican Creole/Jamaican English linguistic review; autistic/AAC-user review; caregiver usability testing; child-facing usability testing under appropriate oversight.
