# WAPS Source Register
Reviewed 26 September 2026. Sources guide high-level caregiver-support and AAC principles; WAPS does not reproduce proprietary assessment items or commercial symbol systems.

1. World Health Organization. *Caregiver skills training for families of children with developmental delays or disabilities – Introduction* (2022). https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240048836
   - Family-centred caregiver skills; everyday play and home routines; communication and engagement.
2. World Health Organization. *Caregiver Skills Training – Adaptation and implementation guide* (2022). https://www.who.int/southeastasia/publications/i/item/9789240048850
   - Contextual and cultural adaptation.
3. World Health Organization. *Caregiver Skills Training – Participants’ guide: group sessions 1–9* (2022). https://www.who.int/southeastasia/publications/i/item/9789240048911
   - Caregiver-facing strategies and goal-setting structure.
4. American Speech-Language-Hearing Association. *Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC), Practice Portal*. https://www.asha.org/Practice-Portal/Professional-Issues/Augmentative-and-Alternative-Communication/
   - Multimodal AAC; functional communication; aided/unaided options.
5. American Speech-Language-Hearing Association. *Autism and Autism Spectrum Disorder, Practice Portal*. https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/autism/
   - Communication support and neurodiversity considerations.
6. Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists. *Augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) – guidance* (updated May 2024). https://www.rcslt.org/members/clinical-guidance/augmentative-and-alternative-communication/augmentative-and-alternative-communication-guidance/
   - Person-centred AAC, communication partners, vocabulary and multimodal communication.

## Implementation boundaries
- WAPS is caregiver-guided communication support, not a standardized assessment or diagnosis.
- Critical self-advocacy communication remains available without earning access.
- Jamaican Creole must not be scored as incorrect Standard English.
- External professional review is required before claiming clinical validation.
